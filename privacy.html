Tellal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Resul İLHAN
Contact: resulilhan0238@gmail.com

In short

  • Tellal has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Tellal contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. One exception: Google ML Kit, used to translate stories in another language on your device, sends Google metrics about the feature's performance and usage (never story text; see 2d). On iPhone, the optional “Check more often” also uses a random device identifier from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (see 5).
  • Your preferences (interests, city, notification settings), your notes and your reading history stay on your device. The learning feed, “While you were away” and “The Week in Review” are computed entirely on your device. So are prayer times.
  • We, the publisher, do not collect or store personal data about you on our servers.

2. Downloading news

Tellal downloads news directly from publishers' public RSS feeds, and images from publishers' servers. These connections go from your device to the publisher, so — as with visiting any website — the publisher can see your IP address and the app's version (User-Agent). How publishers handle that is governed by their own privacy policies.

When you tap a story, the full article opens on the publisher's website: on Android inside your browser (Custom Tabs), on iPhone in Safari. Cookies and privacy rules on that page are the publisher's and your browser's.

Official announcements in the City panel are downloaded the same way, directly from municipalities' public RSS feeds.

2a. Weather (City panel)

If you have chosen a city, its weather comes from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com):

  • First the city's name is sent to Open-Meteo's place search; then the coordinates of the city centre are sent for the forecast. These are your chosen city's centre, not your location.
  • The connection goes from your device to Open-Meteo, which — as with any web request — sees your IP address. Terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.
  • The result is kept on your device for up to 30 minutes. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), credited in the app.

No weather request is made if you have not chosen a city. Prayer times need no request at all: they are computed on your device for your chosen city's centre, the way Diyanet computes them.

2b. Voice briefing

When you tap “Listen”, the headlines and short summaries of the briefing are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine selected on your phone (on Android TextToSpeech, e.g. Google Speech Services; on iPhone iOS's own voices). Tellal does not send this text to any server. Whether your chosen engine processes text on its own servers when a voice is not installed on the device depends on that engine's privacy terms. While it plays, your phone shows playback controls on the lock screen and in notifications.

2c. Sharing

Sharing happens only when you tap “Share”. The link or the card image (headline, source name and the Tellal logo; no article text) is prepared on your device and handed to the app you pick (e.g. WhatsApp) through your phone's share sheet. Tellal does not record where you share.

2d. Automatic translation (Google ML Kit)

Stories written in a language other than the one you chose have only their headline and short summary translated by Google ML Kit translation, running on your phone:

  • Translation happens on the device; story text is never sent to a server. Full articles are not downloaded or translated.
  • The translation model (about 30 MB per language) is downloaded once from Google's servers, on Wi-Fi only by default. Settings › Language and translation lets you allow mobile data.
  • ML Kit connects to Google to download and update the model, and sends Google metrics about the feature's performance and usage (e.g. device and app information, error codes). These metrics do not include story text. Terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.
  • Translations are stored only in the database on your device and are deleted with the story.
  • If you turn on Settings › Language and translation › “Show stories in their original language”, nothing is translated.

2e. Market strip

If you turn on the exchange and gold strip in the Türkiye tab (or set a price reminder on a note), prices are downloaded from two public sources that need no key:

  • The Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye's indicative rates (tcmb.gov.tr); the strip shows “Source: TCMB”.
  • The open-source currency-api data set (cdn.jsdelivr.net, or its mirror currency-api.pages.dev), for items TCMB does not publish and for weekends.

These requests carry nothing about you; the servers see your IP address, as with any web request. With the strip off and no price reminder, these requests are not made.

3. Location (optional)

If you tap “Use my location” when choosing a city and grant permission:

  • Only your approximate location (city level) is read, once. Precise and background location are never requested.
  • The coordinates are turned into a city name with your phone's geocoding service: on Android Geocoder (depending on the device, through a system service such as Google Play services), on iPhone Apple's geocoding service (CLGeocoder; it reaches Apple's servers).
  • The coordinates are not stored and not sent to us; only the resulting city is saved as a preference.

Granting permission is optional; you can pick your city from the list instead.

4. “Why it matters” AI explanation (optional)

When you tap “Why it matters” on a story:

  • Only that story's headline, short summary and the app language are sent to Tellal's proxy (running on Cloudflare). This text comes from the publisher's public feed and contains nothing about you.
  • The proxy forwards it to an AI service, the Claude model of Anthropic PBC, and returns the answer. Anthropic's data terms: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • To keep costs down, the explanation for a story is cached on the server for 7 days and shown to anyone who asks about the same story. The cache entry is derived from the story text, not from any person.
  • To prevent abuse, the proxy briefly uses the request's IP address to apply a per-minute rate limit; the IP address is not stored or logged.

The explanation is labelled “AI-generated summary. Check the source.” in the app and may contain mistakes. Without a proxy address configured, the button is not shown at all.

5. Notifications and reminders

The daily briefing, interest notifications (if you turn them on), bell alerts for topics you follow (keyword alerts), note reminders and the optional prayer time notification are created on your device; matching runs on your phone against downloaded headlines. Home-screen widgets also show only the on-device cache. On Android, Tellal does not use a notification server or push service; no device identifier is registered anywhere.

“Check more often” on iPhone (Notifications settings, on by default, can be turned off at any time): because iPhone runs the background check rarely, our server sends a silent alert with no content every half hour; when iPhone allows it, Tellal wakes up and checks the news, still on your phone. The alert is delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: Firebase gives your phone a random installation identifier and pairs it with Apple's notification address (the APNs token); when subscribing, technical details such as the device model, OS version, language and time zone also go to Google. Everyone gets the same alert from the same topic: your interests, what you read or follow, and your location are not sent. We keep no device list; the identifier stays with Google, and turning the setting off removes the subscription and the registration. Nothing is registered before the first-run setup is finished.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, use for advertising or share personal data with third parties. Apart from the publisher and municipality connections, the weather and market requests, the ML Kit model download, the Firebase registration on iPhone and the optional AI request described above, the app talks to no other server.

7. Children

Tellal is a general-audience news app. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Data on your device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox.

9. Your rights

Because we keep no personal data about you on our servers, there is no account record to delete or export. You can delete the data on your device at any time as described above. For questions under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK (Law No. 6698), contact us at resulilhan0238@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date, and mention significant changes in the app's release notes.

About the test (β) version: the personal test build, which is not in the store, also has the Keşif Defteri (idea notes, feature usage counts and crash records; on the device only, going nowhere unless you export and share them), an update check (release information from GitHub once a day; nothing about you in the request) and bringing data over from Pusula (from Pusula on the same phone or from a file you choose; no server involved).
