Frequently asked

Notifications do not arrive

Check that notifications are on in Settings › Notifications and look at the quiet hours. On Android, battery saving can stop the background check; “Notifications not arriving?” on the same screen shows the steps for your phone's maker. On iPhone the system schedules the background check, so notifications may come less often.

I don't want to see a source

“Hide source” in a card's menu, or Settings › Sources.

Moving my data to another phone

Settings › Move data › Export; Import on the new phone.
